TENDER DOCUMENT, BILL OF QUANTITIES

Tender Reference : MHI/EE/2026/SUBSTATION-04
Work             : Supply and installation of electrical works, 33/11 kV substation
Issuing Division : Electrical Engineering Division
Date of Issue    : 12 January 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4, SCHEDULE OF ITEMS AND QUANTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------------

Item 1: Supply of PVC insulated single core copper conductor cable,
1100 V grade, 2.5 sq mm, for internal panel wiring and control
circuits within the switchgear room. Conductor to be annealed
electrolytic copper. Insulation to be heat resistant PVC suitable
for continuous operation at 70 degrees C.
Quantity: 4,200 metres

Item 2: Supply of Ordinary Portland Cement, 43 grade, in 50 kg
bags, for reinforced concrete foundations of transformer plinths
and equipment supporting structures. Cement to be from a single
approved source for the whole work.
Quantity: 840 bags

Item 3: Supply of hot rolled medium and high tensile structural
steel sections, angles and channels, for fabrication of equipment
support structures, cable trench covers and gantry members.
Quantity: 18.5 metric tonnes

Item 4: Supply of galvanized mild steel tubes, medium grade,
50 mm nominal bore, for the substation water supply line and
fire-fighting ring main, including screwed and socketed joints.
Quantity: 620 metres

Item 5: Supply of industrial safety helmets for site personnel,
non-vented shell, with adjustable harness and chin strap, for use
by all workers within the substation construction area.
Quantity: 150 numbers

Item 6: Supply of unplasticized PVC pipes for underground cable
ducting and drainage runs across the switchyard, 110 mm outer
diameter, suitable for buried application.
Quantity: 950 metres
