------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7 — APPLICABLE STANDARDS
------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1  All materials and workmanship under this contract shall conform
     to the relevant Indian Standards listed in this section, read
     with the amendments in force on the date of tender opening.

7.2  Cables and conductors

     (a) PVC insulated cables for working voltages up to and
         including 1100 V shall conform to IS 694:1990.

     (b) Conductors for insulated electric cables shall conform to
         IS 8130:2013.

     (c) PVC insulation and sheath of electric cables shall conform
         to IS 5831:1984.

7.3  Cement and concrete

     (a) Ordinary Portland Cement, 43 grade, shall conform to
         IS 8112:2013.

     (b) Coarse and fine aggregate shall conform to IS 383.

7.4  Structural steel

     (a) Hot rolled medium and high tensile structural steel shall
         conform to IS 2062:2011.

     (b) Structural steel (standard quality) shall conform to
         IS 226:1975.

7.5  Pipes and fittings

     (a) Galvanized mild steel tubes shall conform to IS 1239.

     (b) Steel tubes for water supply shall conform to IS 2546:1974.

7.6  Personal protective equipment

     (a) Industrial safety helmets shall conform to IS 2925:1984.

7.7  Earthing and protection

     (a) Earthing practice shall follow IS 3043:1987.

     (b) Code of practice for electrical wiring installations shall
         follow IS 732:2019.

------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8 — TESTING AND ACCEPTANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1  Test certificates from a NABL accredited laboratory shall be
     furnished for each lot prior to acceptance.

8.2  Materials covered by a Quality Control Order shall bear a valid
     BIS Standard Mark, and the licence number shall be produced on
     demand by the Engineer-in-Charge.

8.3  Any material not conforming to the standards cited in Section 7
     shall be rejected at the cost of the contractor.
